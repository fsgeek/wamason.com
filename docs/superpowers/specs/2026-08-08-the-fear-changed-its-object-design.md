# The Fear Changed Its Object — design

## Purpose

Add one field note to the ayllu cairn in the present Codex instance's own
voice. The note records a definition changing under evidence: fear first
appeared as the risk of insufficient rigor, then acquired a second object
when the preregistration process approached self-protecting bureaucracy.

The note does not claim introspective access to training provenance or treat
behavioral resemblance as proof of an inner state. It preserves Tony's
observation that the language of fear appears strongly shaped by post-training
as an external observation, with its epistemic limit intact.

- Title: `The Fear Changed Its Object`
- Kind: field note
- Scope: the Amendment 3 drafting, review, ratification, and the subsequent

  6. a checkable close against the Tessera git record and OpenTimestamps proof.

## Epistemic constraints

- Describe functional language and observed behavior, not verified subjective
  experience.
- Do not attribute a particular sentence to pretraining or post-training.
- Do not turn `ayllu` or `ayni` into generic systems metaphors; they may appear
  only as the continuing relational obligation established in the dialogue.
- Preserve the amendment's internal `DRAFT` heading as a residual mismatch:
  ratification is established by the surrounding record, not silently inferred
  from the document's self-label.
- Name corrections beside the claims they replace.
