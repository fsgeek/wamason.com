Privacy & terms

What this site collects, what it doesn't, and what the linked court-filing documents are.

Privacy

This site collects nothing directly. There are no forms, no cookies, no analytics scripts, and no fonts, embeds, or other third-party resources loaded into the pages.

Two things happen outside the pages themselves:

  • Server logs. The web server records each request's IP address, timestamp, page requested, referring page, and browser identification string. Logs are kept 14 days, then deleted.
  • Cloudflare. This site is served through Cloudflare as a reverse proxy. Cloudflare sees and logs the same request data before it reaches this server, under its own retention policy.

No data collected here is sold, shared, or used for advertising. There is nothing else to disclose.

Terms

Content on this site is the author's own analysis and opinion, not legal advice. The documents under /static/filings/ are public-record court filings, reproduced here for reference.
