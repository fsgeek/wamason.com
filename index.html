Every technical system rests on an assumption its builders have stopped seeing. My work is finding the one that is both load-bearing and wrong, then building and proving what should replace it.

Systems researcher and technical expert with twenty-two years of commercial work on Windows file systems and storage internals. PhD from UBC (2025) under Margo Seltzer and Ada Gavrilovska. Twelve issued US patents; co-author of lex & yacc (O'Reilly, 1990) and Windows NT Device Driver Development (New Riders, 1998). Current work spans LLM reliability and observability research, AI governance for financial services, and ontology construction for regulated decision systems.
